[**Crayon to Car**](https://ewels.github.io/tesla-wrap-drawings/) turns kids' drawings into Tesla wraps. They colour in a printed wrap template, you take a photo, and the app finds the template in the photo, lines it up, cuts out the parts that show on the car and saves a PNG for the car's Paint Shop.

1. Print a colouring sheet from the site (or Tesla's own template) and colour it in.
2. Photograph it and drop the photo on the page.
3. Download the PNG, check it on a 3D model of the car, and send it to the car.

Everything runs in the browser. Photos never leave the device.

![The app: a hatched drop area for the photo, with a how-it-works list beside it](/images/projects/crayon-to-car_app.png)

- **Automatic alignment.** Works out which car the drawing is for, and copes with rotated, tilted and shadowed photos.
- **Tesla-ready PNG.** Keeps the template's transparency exactly, removes the printed outlines and stays under Tesla's 1 MB limit.
- **3D preview.** See the drawing on the car before you send it.
- **Better colouring sheets.** A4 landscape, with the parts that never show on the car hatched out and the instructions printed alongside. A printed mark in the glass roof tells the app which car it is and where the drawing sits, so the photo lines up first time.
- **Installs on your phone.** Add it to the home screen and it works like an app, offline too.

![The Model Y colouring sheet: A4 landscape, the template on the left with off-car areas hatched in blue, and instructions on the right](/images/projects/crayon-to-car_sheet.png)
