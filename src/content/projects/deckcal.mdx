[**DeckCal**](https://ewels.github.io/DeckCal/) is a [Stream Deck](https://www.elgato.com/stream-deck) plugin that turns a key into a live indicator for your Google Calendar.

The key counts down to your next meeting, or shows the time remaining in the one you're in, with a progress bar across the top, a yellow fill in the last few minutes before it starts, and a footer band for out-of-office or focus-time overlaps. Press the key to join the meeting.

## Multi-key sweep

Place several countdown keys next to each other and the yellow imminent-fill bar sweeps across them as a single band in the last few minutes before a meeting — a much more visible cue than a single key can give on its own.

## Features

- **Live countdown and progress bar.** Time remaining to (or within) a meeting, with a coloured bar that fills as the meeting elapses.
- **Press to join.** Pressing opens the current Google Meet, Zoom, or Teams meeting. Long press opens the meeting's first attached doc.
- **Multiple Google accounts.** Sign in with one or more accounts and tick exactly which calendars feed the countdown.
- **Special events.** Out-of-office and focus-time events get their own footer band so you can still see your next regular meeting.

Screenshots of every key state, installation instructions and the privacy policy are on the [DeckCal website](https://ewels.github.io/DeckCal/). The source code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/ewels/deckcal).
